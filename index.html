RIFT RECON a multi-agent research team for real League of Legends match data

Ask the match data, not the meta.

Rift Recon answers League of Legends questions with evidence from 4,044 real games (40,410 player-rows) played on the EU Nordic & East server, mostly January 2025 on patch 15.1, joined with Riot's Data Dragon item and champion tables. A Lead plans the research, several Researchers query the data with SQL in parallel, and a Writer turns their findings into a report where every number is cited to the query that produced it.

Who it's for

Players, coaches and analysts who want to test a belief ("control wards win games", "red side is cursed", "Jinx is overrated") against real games. You'll also be told plainly when a question can't be answered from this data.

How to use it

  1. Ask a question, or pick an example below.
  2. If it's too vague to measure, the Lead asks you to narrow it. Click an option or reply.
  3. Watch the agents work on the right: every tool call, its SQL and its result.
  4. Click a Q4 citation to see the exact query behind a number.
